Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information VoiceClone Studio collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. VoiceClone Studio is a desktop application that creates private AI voice clones and generates speech from text on the user's own computer.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account name and email address — collected only when you choose "Sign in with Google". This is used solely to identify your account inside the app.
  • App settings and preferences — stored in a local database on your computer.

We request only the basic Google scopes openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, files, contacts, photos, or any other Google data.

2. Audio and voice data

  • All audio recordings, uploaded samples, voice models and generated speech are processed and stored locally on your own computer.
  • Your audio is never uploaded to us, to Google, or to any third-party server.
  • Deleting the app (or its data folder) permanently removes all recordings and voice models.

3. How your information is used

  • To sign you in and recognize your account between sessions.
  • To secure the app and enforce administrator roles.
  • To provide technical support if you contact us.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties.
  • We do not collect or listen to your audio.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Account identity (name and email) is kept only while your account exists in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account data at any time by emailing us; it will be removed from the app's database.
  • All audio and voice models remain under your control on your device and can be deleted by you directly.

6. Children's privacy

VoiceClone Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
